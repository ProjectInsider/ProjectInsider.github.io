About / ProjectInsider

Built for the Windows community.

We believe great design deserves to be remembered — and explored again.

Modern code.
Classic soul.

ProjectInsider is an independent community project inspired by the Windows 8 and early Windows 10 eras. Our goal is to create software experiences that feel at home on those systems.

MetroStore is our Windows Store-inspired project. It aims to bring the classic Metro app discovery experience to a range of Windows versions, with an interface and behavior tailored to each release.

This site is a preview. Features, editions and download availability may change during development.

Our approach

Native-inspired design

Version-specific clients

Each planned MetroStore edition is designed around the characteristics of its Windows generation.
