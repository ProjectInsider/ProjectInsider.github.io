MetroStore

MetroStore is a community-made app store inspired by the original Windows 8 Store. It brings back the clean Metro experience with large featured areas, flat tiles, Segoe UI typography and a growing collection of archived Windows apps.

The goal is simple: make discovering classic Windows apps feel like using a real Microsoft Store from the Metro era again.

About MetroStore

The Windows 8 Store experience, brought back to life.

MetroStore is designed around the visual language of the Windows 8 Store: strong typography, open white space, colorful content tiles and horizontal discovery. Instead of feeling like a simple archive folder, the project presents apps through a real storefront with Featured and Spotlight areas, categories and a large app library.

Metro design

Flat layouts, square corners, Segoe UI and large content areas recreate the look and feeling of Microsoft's Windows 8 era.

App discovery

Featured content, Spotlight sections and categories give users a visual way to discover apps instead of browsing a plain list of files.

Archive focused

MetroStore gives archived Windows app packages a proper storefront-style home, helping classic Metro apps feel alive and discoverable again.
